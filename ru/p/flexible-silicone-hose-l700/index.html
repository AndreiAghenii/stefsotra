Stefsotra Silicone Corrugated Hose L700mm – High-Performance and Flexible Solution for Fluid Systems

Stefsotra silicone corrugated hoses (L700mm) are engineered using the latest technology to provide superior flexibility, durability, and reliability in fluid transfer applications. Made from high-stability silicone, these hoses are designed to recover after multiple heating and cooling cycles, ensuring long-lasting performance in extreme conditions. Ideal for use in automotive, industrial, and heavy machinery systems, Stefsotra silicone corrugated hoses offer a robust solution for coolant systems, turbocharging, and various fluid management tasks.

Key Features:

  • High-Quality Silicone Construction: Manufactured from premium, high-stability silicone, these hoses are engineered to withstand repeated heating and cooling cycles without losing flexibility or strength.
  • Corrugated Design: The flexible, corrugated structure allows these hoses to bend and stretch without compromising their durability, making them perfect for installations in tight spaces.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistance: These hoses are designed to operate efficiently in extreme temperatures ranging from -60°C to +180°C, making them suitable for a wide range of demanding applications.
  • Chemical Resistance: Silicone is chemically inert, offering excellent resistance to weakly aggressive media such as coolant, antifreeze, and hot and cold water.
  • Versatile Applications: Ideal for use in:
    • Cooling systems and turbocharging for commercial vehicles
    • Mainline systems for additional heating (in vehicles and buses)
    • Creating custom hot and cold water systems (heat carriers)
    • Replacing old rubber hoses in household appliances with modern silicone alternatives
    • Maintenance of tracked and wheeled machinery (excavators, bulldozers)
    • Motorcycle and buggy construction
    • Car tuning (sports and special vehicles)
  • Operating Conditions: Stefsotra silicone hoses perform excellently under harsh conditions, whether in the extreme cold of the northern regions or the intense heat of the south. Their durability in both hot and cold environments makes them suitable for a variety of industries and applications.
  • Working Pressure: Suitable for working pressures ranging from 5 to 40 kg/cm², depending on diameter, these hoses work efficiently under standard turbocharger air pressures of 1.5 kg/cm², and are able to withstand rapid changes in pressure during “kick-down” or "turbo-holes."

Installation and Use:

  • Clamping: These hoses are typically secured with standard worm clamps, ideally with a band width of at least 12 mm.
  • Not Suitable For: Diesel fuel and gasoline.

For industries requiring flexible, durable, and heat-resistant fluid transfer solutions, Stefsotra silicone corrugated hoses (L700mm) provide the reliability and long-term performance needed for a wide range of automotive, industrial, and heavy machinery applications.
