Stefsotra Food-Grade Suction Hoses – Safe and Reliable for Liquid and Viscous Food Product Transfer

Stefsotra Food-Grade Suction Hoses are specially designed for the transfer, suction, and discharge of liquid and viscous food products. Made from high-quality, food-safe materials, these hoses comply with international safety standards and are ideal for a wide range of food processing applications. Our Food-Grade Suction Hoses ensure hygienic, safe, and efficient fluid handling without compromising human health or the environment.

Key Features:

  • Food-Safe Materials: These suction hoses are manufactured from premium, non-toxic materials that comply with global food safety standards, ensuring safe contact with food and beverage products.
  • Versatile Applications: Perfect for use in the food and beverage industry, including applications for the transfer of liquid and viscous food products, such as juices, syrups, sauces, dairy products, and more.
  • Durability and Flexibility: Designed for heavy-duty applications, these hoses offer excellent durability, resistance to wear, and flexibility, making them suitable for both suction and discharge operations.
  • Environmentally Safe: Stefsotra Food-Grade Suction Hoses are not harmful to the environment or human health, providing a safe, eco-friendly solution for food handling needs.
  • Temperature Resistance: These hoses can handle a wide range of temperatures, making them ideal for both cold and hot food product applications.

Applications:

  • Food and Beverage Industry: For the safe transfer, suction, and discharge of liquids, syrups, dairy products, sauces, and other viscous food products.
  • Food Processing: Used in various food processing lines for efficient material handling.
  • Beverage Production: Ideal for the handling of liquids, including juices, soft drinks, and other beverage ingredients.
  • Pharmaceutical and Cosmetic Industries: Suitable for industries requiring hygienic handling of liquid or viscous substances.

Benefits:

  • Safe for Food Contact: Meets international food safety standards, ensuring non-toxicity and safe use with food products.
  • Reliable and Durable: Resistant to wear and tear, offering long-lasting performance in demanding environments.
  • Eco-Friendly: Made from non-toxic, environmentally safe materials, contributing to sustainable practices in the food industry.
  • Flexible and Easy to Use: Designed for easy handling and installation in various food and beverage processing systems.
