Stefsotra Food-Grade Technical Plate – Safe, Eco-Friendly Material for Food and Pharmaceutical Contact

Stefsotra Food-Grade Technical Plates are the most environmentally safe and reliable material for use in applications that come into contact with food products or pharmaceuticals. Made from high-quality materials, these plates meet stringent safety standards and are designed for use in the food and beverage industry, as well as in the pharmaceutical sector. They are ideal for use in containers, tanks, and vessels used to store or transport food and medical products.

Key Features:

  • Food and Pharmaceutical Safety: Specifically designed for safe contact with food products, pharmaceuticals, and medical supplies, these plates ensure hygienic conditions for storage and transportation.
  • Versatile Applications: Perfect for use in containers, tanks, and vessels that hold or transport food or pharmaceutical products. They can withstand conditions in industries such as food processing, beverage production, and pharmaceuticals.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistant: Suitable for environments with pressure up to 0.6 MPa and temperatures ranging from -30°C to +140°C, making them adaptable to various processing conditions.
  • Eco-Friendly: Made from environmentally safe materials, these plates contribute to sustainable practices in the food and pharmaceutical industries.
  • Durability: Highly resistant to wear and tear, ensuring a long service life in demanding environments.

Types of Food-Grade Technical Plates:

  1. Type 1: Designed for use with alcoholic beverages, such as wine, vodka, cognac, and other spirits.
  2. Type 2: Suitable for dairy products like milk, cream, fermented milk products, drinking water, and kvass.
  3. Type 3: Ideal for products like fruits, vegetables, fruit and vegetable juices, purees, preserves, beer, yeast suspensions, sugar syrups, and other non-alcoholic beverages.
  4. Type 4: For use with fresh meat, fish, meat and fish products, canned vegetables, mushrooms, and other similar items.

Applications:

Stefsotra Food-Grade Technical Plates are widely used in industries where the safe handling of food, beverages, and pharmaceuticals is critical:

  • Food Processing: In the production and storage of a wide variety of food products, including beverages, dairy, and meat products.
  • Beverage Industry: For use in tanks, vessels, and containers that store or transport alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages.
  • Pharmaceutical Industry: Ensures safe handling and storage of pharmaceuticals and medical products.
  • Medical Equipment: For the production of seals and other components that come into contact with food or pharmaceutical materials.

Benefits:

  • Safe for Food and Pharma Contact: Complies with stringent standards for food and pharmaceutical safety.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistance: Able to function effectively in a wide range of temperatures and pressures.
  • Eco-Friendly and Durable: Made from sustainable materials with a long lifespan, offering value for money.
  • Versatile Usage: Can be used in a wide array of applications in the food, beverage, and pharmaceutical industries.
