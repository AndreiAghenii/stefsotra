Stefsotra Plastering Hoses – Durable, High-Performance Hoses for Abrasive and Building Material Delivery

Stefsotra plastering hoses are designed for exceptional durability and high performance in a variety of demanding applications. Ideal for sandblasting, shot blasting, and surface cleaning, these hoses efficiently deliver abrasive materials like quartz sand, steel shot, corundum, and glass for surface preparation and cleaning. In addition, they are perfect for transporting plaster, gypsum, cement, and concrete through concrete pumps, making them a versatile tool for construction and finishing projects.

Key features of Stefsotra plastering hoses include:

  • High Abrasion Resistance: Specially designed to withstand pressure and wear, ensuring long-term reliability and reduced maintenance costs.
  • Anti-Static Coating: The inner wall is coated with an anti-static layer to prevent material buildup and enhance performance.
  • Multi-Purpose Use: Suitable for both abrasive material delivery and building material transportation, making them ideal for sandblasting and plastering applications.
  • Long-Lasting Quality: Proven over time, Stefsotra plastering hoses are trusted for their superior quality and long service life.

Whether you're working on surface preparation or need reliable hose systems for concrete delivery, Stefsotra plastering hoses offer unmatched quality and durability for professional contractors and construction projects.
