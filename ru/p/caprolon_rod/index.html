Stefsotra Caprolon – High-Performance Engineering Plastic for Industrial Applications

Stefsotra Caprolon is a high-performance engineering plastic made from ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene (UHMW-PE), designed to deliver exceptional durability and reliability in demanding industrial environments. Available in rod form with a diameter of 2 inches and a length of 1 foot, it is widely used for creating components such as gears, bearings, bushings, and washers. Known for its superior strength, wear resistance, and low friction properties, Caprolon is an ideal choice for industries that require tough, long-lasting materials for mechanical systems.

Key Features:

  • Material: Ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene (UHMW-PE), offering excellent mechanical strength and wear resistance.
  • Dimensions: Rod form, 2-inch diameter, with a length of 1 foot. Diameter tolerance: (-0.000" - +0.008"); Length tolerance: (-1.000" - +1.000").
  • Color: White, opaque clarity.
  • Tensile Strength: 4400 psi, providing strong performance under stress.
  • Impact Toughness: 16.0 foot-pounds/inch, rated excellent for resistance to impact.
  • Friction Coefficient: 0.15, rated as very slippery for minimal friction in moving parts.
  • Water Absorption: 0.0001%, rated excellent for moisture resistance.
  • Density: 0.034 lb/cubic inch, ensuring lightweight and efficient components.
  • Hardness: 70D, offering a solid balance of rigidity and flexibility.
  • Temperature Range: Functional from -70°F to 180°F, suitable for a wide range of operational conditions.
  • Compliance: Meets FDA and UL 94 HB standards for safety and reliability in food processing and other regulated industries.

Stefsotra Caprolon’s unique combination of strength, low friction, and resistance to abrasion, chemicals, and temperature extremes make it a top choice for creating high-performance mechanical components. Its superior durability ensures long-term reliability in a variety of industries, including automotive, chemical processing, manufacturing, and heavy machinery.
