Описание от производителя

Текст ниже — от производителя, перевод пока не выполнен.

The LAYFLAT Hose is designed for industrial use as a distribution pipeline. This seamless, nylon-reinforced hose is perfect for organizing irrigation (watering) systems in agriculture. Made from high-quality polymer materials, it ensures no leakage or delamination during operation. The hose is UV-resistant, easy to lay out at the start of the season, and can be retracted at the end of the season for storage. It operates under a working pressure of 8.0 bar and comes in a 50-meter coil.

Specifications:

  • Length: 50 meters
  • Working Pressure: 8.0 bar
  • Material: Reinforced with nylon thread
  • Service Life: Up to 5 years
  • UV Resistance: Yes
  • Application: Ideal for agricultural irrigation systems
  • Features: Easy deployment, storage, and installation with special fittings for drip irrigation systems
