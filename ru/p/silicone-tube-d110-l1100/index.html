Stefsotra Silicone 90° Elbow Hose L102*102mm – Durable, High-Performance Fluid Transfer Solution

The Stefsotra Silicone 90° Elbow Hose (L102*102mm) is designed to provide reliable and flexible fluid transfer in systems where a 90° angle transition is needed. Manufactured from advanced, high-stability silicone, this hose is built to withstand high temperatures and harsh conditions. The internal layer is reinforced with high-strength polyamide fabric, providing extra durability and resistance to pressure. Ideal for automotive, industrial, and heating applications, this hose ensures smooth fluid flow and long-lasting performance.

Key Features:

  • Premium Silicone Construction: Made from high-quality, high-stability silicone, this hose recovers after multiple heating and cooling cycles, ensuring long-term durability and flexibility.
  • Reinforced with Polyamide Fabric: The inner part of the hose is reinforced with high-strength polyamide fabric, providing excellent resistance to pressure, wear, and tear in demanding environments.
  • 90° Elbow Design: Ideal for systems requiring fluid transfer at a 90° angle, this hose ensures smooth redirection of fluids without loss of performance, even in tight spaces.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistance: Withstanding temperatures from -60°C to +180°C, this hose is suitable for a variety of applications, including cooling systems and turbocharging. It can handle working pressures from 5 to 40 kg/cm², depending on the diameter.
  • Easy to Install: Compatible with standard hose clamps (12mm width or more) for a secure, leak-free fit.

Applications:

Stefsotra Silicone 90° Elbow Hose (L102*102mm) is ideal for use in:

  • Automotive Cooling and Turbocharging Systems: Perfect for use in heavy-duty vehicle cooling systems, including trucks, buses, and other commercial vehicles.
  • HVAC Systems: Suitable for use in heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems requiring flexible and durable hoses for fluid transfer.
  • Industrial Machinery: Used in various industrial applications where a 90° angle transition is needed to manage fluid flow efficiently.
  • Replacement of Rubber Hoses: Excellent for replacing outdated rubber hoses in household appliances, industrial equipment, and construction machinery like excavators and bulldozers.
  • Motorsports and Custom Builds: Ideal for custom vehicle builds, off-road vehicles, and high-performance cars requiring a reliable hose for fluid transfer.
  • Vehicle Tuning: Great for use in vehicle tuning projects, especially for turbocharged, performance, or special-purpose cars.

Operating Conditions:

  • Temperature Range: Operates in a wide range of temperatures, from -60°C to +180°C, making it suitable for both hot and cold fluids, including coolant, antifreeze, and water.
  • Pressure Range: The hose can handle working pressures ranging from 5 to 40 kg/cm², making it ideal for use in turbocharging systems or other high-pressure environments.

Installation and Use:

  • Hose Clamps: For a secure connection, use standard worm-drive hose clamps with a recommended width of at least 12mm. The hose provides a reliable and leak-free fit when properly installed.
  • Turbocharging Systems: Works effectively in turbocharging systems at standard pressures of up to 1.5 kg/cm² and can withstand pressure surges during "kick-down" or "turbo lag" scenarios.

Important Notes:

  • Not Suitable for Diesel or Gasoline: The hose is not recommended for use with diesel fuel or gasoline.
  • Stretchable: While flexible, the hose may stretch slightly during use (up to 1-2mm), which is normal for silicone hoses.

Stefsotra Silicone 90° Elbow Hose (L102*102mm) is a cost-effective, durable solution for fluid transfer in a wide range of automotive, industrial, and HVAC applications. Its high-quality construction, reinforced with polyamide fabric, provides excellent resistance to pressure, wear, and high temperatures, ensuring reliable performance in even the most demanding environments.
