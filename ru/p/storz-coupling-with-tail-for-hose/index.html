Stefsotra STORZ Coupling with Tail for Hose – Reliable and Secure Hose Connection

Stefsotra STORZ coupling with tail for hose is designed for quick, secure connections in a variety of fluid handling applications. These couplings feature a tail specifically designed to fit hoses, ensuring a tight, leak-proof seal for efficient fluid transfer. Made from high-quality materials, Stefsotra STORZ couplings provide exceptional durability and are easy to use, making them ideal for firefighting, industrial systems, irrigation, and other high-pressure environments.

Key Features:

  • Quick and Secure Connection: The STORZ coupling system allows for fast, tool-free connections and disconnections, making it ideal for emergency and high-pressure applications.
  • Tail for Hose Compatibility: Equipped with a specially designed tail that fits securely into hoses, preventing leaks and ensuring reliable fluid transfer.
  • Durable and Corrosion-Resistant: Made from robust materials, such as aluminum or stainless steel, for long-lasting use in harsh environments.
  • Wide Range of Applications: Perfect for use in firefighting equipment, industrial pipelines, irrigation systems, and high-pressure fluid transfer.
  • Leak-Proof Design: Ensures a tight, reliable seal for preventing leaks during operation, even under pressure.

For fast, secure hose connections, Stefsotra STORZ couplings with tail offer the durability, ease of use, and reliability required for both emergency services and industrial fluid handling applications.
