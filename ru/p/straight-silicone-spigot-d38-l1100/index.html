Stefsotra Silicone Elbow Hose with Transition 45° L102*102mm – Durable and Flexible Fluid Transfer Solution

The Stefsotra Silicone Elbow Hose with a 45° transition (L102*102mm) is designed for high-performance fluid transfer applications, where space constraints and directional fluid flow are important. Manufactured using cutting-edge technology, this hose is made from high-stability silicone, offering exceptional resistance to temperature extremes, wear, and chemical degradation. With reinforced polyamide fabric inside, these hoses are perfect for use in automotive, industrial, and HVAC systems, ensuring smooth, leak-free performance.

Key Features:

  • Premium Silicone Construction: Made from advanced high-stability silicone, these hoses recover efficiently after repeated heating and cooling cycles, maintaining their structural integrity over time.
  • Reinforced Polyamide Fabric Lining: The interior of the hose is lined with high-strength polyamide fabric, enhancing durability and pressure resistance, making them suitable for demanding environments.
  • 45° Transition Design: The hose features a 45° angled transition, ideal for fluid transfer in systems where a change in direction is required. This angle allows for easier installation and optimal fluid flow between components at an angle.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistance: Stefsotra silicone hoses can withstand a temperature range from -60°C to +180°C, ensuring reliable performance in both cold and hot environments. They can handle working pressures ranging from 5 to 40 kg/cm², depending on the diameter.
  • Wall Thickness: These hoses feature a durable 4mm wall thickness, providing extra strength and resistance to mechanical stress, pressure, and wear.
  • Versatile Fluid Compatibility: Suitable for a wide variety of fluids, including:
    • Antifreeze
    • Coolant
    • Cold and Hot Water
    • Other weakly aggressive fluids

Applications:

  • Automotive Cooling Systems: Ideal for turbocharging, cooling, and fluid transfer applications in trucks, buses, and other heavy-duty vehicles.
  • Industrial Applications: Used in machinery, heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems where flexibility and pressure resistance are required.
  • Repairs and Replacements: Suitable for replacing outdated rubber hoses in household appliances, industrial machinery, and heavy equipment like excavators and bulldozers.
  • Motorsports & Tuning: Great for custom vehicle builds, including tuning of sports cars and special vehicles, and construction of off-road buggies.

Operating Conditions:

  • Extreme Durability: Stefsotra silicone hoses maintain excellent performance even in harsh conditions, from the extreme cold of northern climates to the heat of southern regions.
  • Temperature Range: Operates effectively from -60°C to +180°C.
  • Maximum Pressure: These hoses can withstand working pressures from 5 to 40 kg/cm², ensuring secure fluid transfer even under high-pressure conditions.

Installation and Use:

  • These hoses can be easily installed using standard hose clamps, with a recommended clamp width of 12mm or more, ensuring a secure, leak-free fit.
  • The 45° transition design allows for smooth, efficient fluid flow without restrictions, even when navigating tight spaces or directional changes in the system.

Important Notes:

  • Not suitable for: Diesel fuel and gasoline.
  • Pressure Handling: These hoses are designed to handle standard pressure in turbocharging systems (1.5 kg/cm²) and can withstand pressure surges, such as those experienced during "kick-down" or "turbo-lag" scenarios.

Stefsotra Silicone Elbow Hose with Transition 45° L102*102mm provides a flexible, durable, and efficient solution for a wide range of automotive, industrial, and machinery applications, ensuring long-lasting performance and secure fluid transfer in both high and low-temperature environments.
