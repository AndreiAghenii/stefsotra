tefsotra Silicone Elbow Hose with Transition 90° L102*102mm – High-Performance Solution for Fluid Transfer

The Stefsotra Silicone Elbow Hose with 90° transition (L102*102mm) is designed to provide a reliable and efficient fluid transfer solution in systems where a 90-degree angle is required for optimal flow direction. Made from high-quality, high-stability silicone, these hoses are engineered to withstand extreme temperatures and maintain flexibility and durability under pressure. They are ideal for use in automotive, industrial, and HVAC applications, ensuring smooth, secure, and efficient fluid transfer.

Key Features:

  • Premium Silicone Construction: Made from advanced high-stability silicone, these hoses can recover from repeated heating and cooling cycles, ensuring long-lasting performance and resistance to wear and chemical degradation.
  • Reinforced Polyamide Fabric Lining: The inner part of the hose is lined with high-strength polyamide fabric, offering increased durability and pressure resistance, making it ideal for demanding environments.
  • 90° Elbow Transition: This hose features a 90° angle, making it perfect for applications requiring a sharp directional change. It ensures a smooth transition between system components and provides secure, leak-free fluid transfer.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistance: Stefsotra silicone hoses are engineered to handle a temperature range from -60°C to +180°C, making them suitable for both cold and hot environments. They can withstand pressures ranging from 5 to 40 kg/cm², depending on the diameter, ensuring optimal fluid flow in high-pressure systems.
  • Wall Thickness: With a wall thickness of 4mm, these hoses offer enhanced durability, pressure resistance, and wear resistance, making them ideal for a variety of fluid transfer needs.
  • Versatile Fluid Compatibility: Suitable for:
    • Antifreeze
    • Coolant
    • Cold and Hot Water
    • Other weakly aggressive fluids

Applications:
Stefsotra Silicone Elbow Hoses with 90° Transition (L102*102mm) are perfect for use in:

  • Automotive Systems: Used in the cooling systems and turbocharging setups of trucks, buses, and other heavy-duty vehicles.
  • Industrial Applications: Suitable for use in industrial fluid transfer systems, machinery cooling, and HVAC installations.
  • Repair and Replacement: Ideal for replacing outdated rubber hoses in household appliances, industrial equipment, and construction machinery such as excavators and bulldozers.
  • Motorsports and Vehicle Tuning: Great for custom builds, off-road buggies, and vehicle tuning, including for sports and special-purpose vehicles.

Operating Conditions:

  • Temperature Range: These hoses perform reliably in extreme temperatures, from -60°C to +180°C, making them suitable for both cold and hot water systems, coolant transfer, and other high-performance applications.
  • Maximum Pressure: The hoses can handle working pressures from 5 to 40 kg/cm², ensuring secure fluid transfer under varying pressures.
  • High Durability: Designed to withstand harsh environments, these silicone hoses maintain excellent performance in both cold northern climates and hot southern regions.

Installation and Use:

  • Easy Installation: These hoses can be installed using standard hose clamps (recommended width 12mm or greater), ensuring a secure and leak-free fit. The 90° transition design allows for easy directional changes in tight spaces or complex fluid transfer systems.
  • Pressure Resistance: These hoses are designed to handle standard turbocharging system pressures (1.5 kg/cm²) and can withstand pressure surges, such as those experienced during kick-downs or turbo lag scenarios.

Important Notes:

  • Not suitable for: Diesel fuel and gasoline.
  • Durability: The hoses offer long-lasting durability and flexibility, providing excellent performance in high-demand systems where reliability and leak-free performance are essential.

Stefsotra Silicone Elbow Hose with 90° Transition (L102*102mm) is a high-performance, flexible, and durable hose that provides an efficient solution for fluid transfer applications requiring a 90-degree directional change. Perfect for automotive, industrial, and machinery applications, these hoses offer exceptional performance under extreme temperature and pressure conditions.
