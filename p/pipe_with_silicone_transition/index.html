Stefsotra Silicone Straight Hose with Transition L102 – High-Performance Solution for Fluid Transfer

Stefsotra silicone straight hoses with transition (L102) are engineered for efficient fluid transfer across systems with varying diameters, making them ideal for automotive, industrial, and machinery applications. Manufactured with advanced silicone technology, these hoses provide superior flexibility, durability, and resistance to extreme temperatures. Designed to handle a wide range of fluids including antifreeze, coolant, and water, they ensure smooth and reliable performance in both hot and cold environments.

Key Features:

  • Premium Silicone Construction: Made from high-stability silicone, these hoses can recover from repeated heating and cooling cycles, ensuring longevity and consistent performance in demanding environments.
  • Reinforced Polyamide Lining: The inner part of the hose is reinforced with high-strength polyamide fabric, providing increased durability, resistance to pressure, and long-term reliability.
  • Transition Design: Smooth transition between different diameters ensures a secure, leak-free connection and optimal fluid flow between systems of varying sizes.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistance: Stefsotra silicone hoses can operate in a temperature range from -50°C to +180°C, making them suitable for a wide variety of fluid transfer applications. They can withstand a maximum pressure of 0.7 MPa.
  • Wall Thickness: These hoses feature a wall thickness of 4mm, providing enhanced durability and resistance to wear, pressure, and mechanical stress.
  • Versatile Fluid Compatibility: These hoses are designed for use with:
    • Antifreeze
    • Coolant
    • Cold and hot water
    • Other weakly aggressive fluids

Applications:
Ideal for use in:

  • Automotive Cooling Systems: Perfect for fluid transfer in automotive systems, including radiators and turbocharging applications.
  • Industrial and Machinery Systems: Suitable for various fluid transfer and cooling systems in machinery, manufacturing, and industrial equipment.
  • HVAC Systems: Can be used in heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems where temperature resistance and reliability are required.
  • Appliance and Equipment Repairs: Ideal for replacing outdated rubber hoses with modern, durable silicone alternatives.

Operating Conditions:

  • Durability: These hoses perform reliably even in extreme cold (-50°C) and heat (+180°C), making them ideal for a wide range of applications in both cold and hot environments.
  • Maximum Pressure: Designed to withstand a maximum working pressure of 0.7 MPa, ensuring secure fluid transfer in high-pressure systems.

Installation:

  • These hoses are easy to install using standard hose clamps and are designed to provide a secure, leak-free fit. The transition design ensures smooth fluid flow without restrictions.

Important Note:

  • Not suitable for use with diesel fuel or gasoline.

Stefsotra silicone straight hoses with transition (L102) are a high-performance, flexible, and durable solution for fluid transfer systems that require smooth transitions between different diameters. Ideal for automotive, industrial, and HVAC applications, these hoses deliver exceptional performance in a wide variety of demanding environments.
