Stefsotra Silicone U-Shaped Hose 180° – Flexible and Durable Fluid Transfer Solution

Stefsotra silicone U-shaped hoses (180°) are designed for efficient fluid transfer in various industrial, commercial, and automotive applications. Made from high-quality silicone, these hoses offer superior flexibility, durability, and resistance to high temperatures, chemicals, and pressure. The 180° U-shape design makes them ideal for tight spaces and complex pipework, providing a smooth and secure connection for fluid systems that require bending or directional flow changes.

Key Features:

  • High-Quality Silicone Construction: Manufactured from premium, food-grade silicone, these hoses are resistant to heat, chemicals, and wear, ensuring long-lasting performance in demanding environments.
  • 180° U-Shape Design: The 180° U-shape allows for easy redirection of fluid flow, making it ideal for applications where space is limited or where a bend in the hose is required.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistance: These hoses are designed to withstand high temperatures (up to 300°F) and pressures, making them suitable for use in both industrial and automotive systems.
  • Non-Reactive and Safe: Silicone is chemically inert, ensuring compatibility with a wide range of fluids without leaching or contamination.
  • Flexible and Lightweight: Despite their durability, these hoses remain highly flexible, easy to install, and suitable for both low and high-pressure applications.
  • Versatile Applications: Perfect for use in automotive systems, HVAC, chemical processing, food and beverage handling, and other fluid transfer systems.

For reliable, flexible, and high-performance fluid transfer, Stefsotra silicone U-shaped hoses (180°) provide the durability, heat resistance, and performance required for a wide range of industrial, automotive, and commercial applications.
