Rubber sheets are widely used in the manufacturing of rubber products for sealing stationary connections, absorbing single impact loads, as well as serving as gaskets and flooring materials. The material offers versatility and reliability in various industrial applications, providing effective sealing, vibration absorption, and protection in environments where durability and flexibility are essential.

Key Features:

  • Applications:
    • Used to produce rubber technical products such as seals for stationary connections.
    • Absorbs shock and impact loads, making it ideal for cushioning and protecting machinery or equipment.
    • Suitable for use as gaskets and industrial flooring.
  • Temperature Range: The rubber sheet is designed to function effectively within a temperature range of -30°C to +80°C.
  • Durability: Offers good wear resistance, making it durable for use in both light and heavy-duty applications.
  • Versatility: Can be used in various industries, including automotive, construction, and manufacturing, where sealing, vibration damping, and impact protection are required.
