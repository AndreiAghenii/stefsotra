Stefsotra Silicone Straight Hose L1000mm – Durable and Flexible Fluid Transfer Solution

Stefsotra silicone straight hoses (L1000mm) are designed for high-performance fluid transfer in a variety of industrial, automotive, and commercial applications. Made from high-quality silicone, these hoses offer excellent flexibility, temperature resistance, and durability. With an internal diameter range from 6mm to 152mm, they can accommodate a wide range of fluid transfer needs. Their straight design and robust construction make them ideal for automotive, machinery, and fluid management systems that require reliable performance under diverse conditions.

Key Features:

  • Premium Silicone Construction: Made from high-stability silicone, these hoses provide excellent resistance to temperature fluctuations, UV exposure, and chemical degradation, ensuring long-term reliability in tough environments.
  • Internal Diameter Options: Available in a wide range of internal diameters from 6mm to 152mm, making them versatile for various fluid systems, from small to large-scale applications.
  • Length and Flexibility: With a standard length of 1000mm, this straight hose provides optimal reach and flexibility for fluid transfer systems, ensuring a secure connection without compromising durability.
  • Color: The hoses are blue, offering a visually distinct and easy-to-identify solution in industrial and automotive environments.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistance: These silicone hoses can withstand extreme temperatures from -60°C to +180°C, making them suitable for both hot and cold water systems, as well as other high-temperature and high-pressure applications.
  • Chemical Resistance: Silicone is chemically inert, ensuring compatibility with a wide range of fluids, including coolants, antifreeze, and water-based solutions.
  • Versatile Applications: Ideal for use in:
    • Automotive Industry: For cooling systems, turbocharging, and fluid transfer in cars, trucks, and other vehicles.
    • Automotive Repair & Service: Used in automotive repair shops for replacing old rubber hoses with more durable silicone alternatives.
    • Machinery and Engineering: In manufacturing and machinery applications that require reliable fluid transfer and temperature resistance.
    • HVAC Systems: Ideal for heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems where durability and resistance to extreme temperatures are crucial.

Operating Conditions:

  • Extreme Durability: Stefsotra silicone straight hoses perform excellently in harsh conditions, from freezing cold environments to hot climates, ensuring reliability in a wide range of industries.
  • Suitable for:
    • Cold and hot water systems
    • Weakly aggressive fluids (coolants, antifreeze)
    • General industrial and automotive applications

Installation and Use:

  • These hoses can be easily installed using standard hose clamps. The straight design provides a simple and effective solution for fluid transfer, ensuring easy installation and leak-free performance in a variety of applications.

Stefsotra silicone straight hoses (L1000mm) are the ideal choice for industries and systems requiring flexible, durable, and heat-resistant hoses for fluid transfer. Their wide range of diameters, temperature resistance, and versatility make them suitable for automotive, machinery, and industrial applications.
