Stefsotra Silicone 135° Elbow Hose L102*102mm – Durable and Flexible Fluid Transfer Solution

The Stefsotra Silicone 135° Elbow Hose (L102*102mm) is designed to provide reliable fluid transfer in systems where a 135° angle transition is required. Manufactured using advanced technology from high-stability silicone, this hose offers excellent flexibility, temperature resistance, and durability. The inner lining is reinforced with high-strength polyamide fabric for enhanced pressure resistance and longevity, making it ideal for automotive, industrial, and HVAC applications.

Key Features:

  • Premium Silicone Construction: Crafted from high-quality, high-stability silicone, the hose recovers after multiple heating and cooling cycles, maintaining its flexibility and strength over time.
  • Reinforced Polyamide Fabric: The interior is lined with strong polyamide fabric, offering additional pressure resistance, durability, and performance under demanding conditions.
  • 135° Elbow Transition: The 135° angle of the hose allows for efficient redirection of fluid flow in systems with tight spaces or complex directional changes, ensuring a smooth and secure fluid transfer.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistance: The silicone construction allows the hose to withstand temperatures ranging from -60°C to +180°C. It can handle working pressures from 5 to 40 kg/cm², depending on the diameter, ensuring reliable performance even under high pressure.
  • Wall Thickness: With a wall thickness of 4mm, these hoses provide superior durability and resistance to wear and tear, ensuring a long service life in harsh conditions.
  • Compatible Fluids: These hoses are compatible with weakly aggressive fluids, including:
    • Antifreeze
    • Coolant
    • Cold Water
    • Hot Water

Applications:
Stefsotra Silicone 135° Elbow Hoses (L102*102mm) are versatile and suitable for use in a wide range of applications, including:

  • Cooling Systems and Turbocharging: Perfect for use in the cooling and turbocharging systems of trucks, buses, and other heavy-duty vehicles.
  • HVAC Systems: Ideal for heating, ventilation, and air conditioning applications where fluid transfer is necessary at a 135° angle.
  • Industrial Machinery: Used in machinery and fluid transfer systems where flexible, durable hoses are required.
  • Repairs and Replacements: Excellent for replacing outdated rubber hoses in household appliances, industrial machinery, and construction vehicles such as excavators and bulldozers.
  • Motorsports and Vehicle Tuning: Great for custom vehicle builds, including tuning of sports and off-road vehicles, as well as constructing buggies.

Operating Conditions:

  • Temperature Range: The hose operates efficiently in temperatures from -60°C to +180°C, making it suitable for both cold and hot water systems, as well as coolant transfer.
  • Maximum Pressure: The silicone hose can withstand working pressures from 5 to 40 kg/cm², ensuring smooth, reliable fluid flow in systems with varying pressure conditions.
  • Durability: Designed to maintain excellent performance in extreme climates, from the coldest regions to the hottest environments.

Installation and Use:

  • Easy Installation: Standard hose clamps (12mm or wider) can be used for secure attachment, ensuring a leak-free connection. The 135° elbow design ensures smooth fluid flow and effective redirection, even in tight spaces or complex systems.
  • Pressure Resistance: Stefsotra silicone hoses are built to handle turbocharging systems with standard pressure (1.5 kg/cm²) and can withstand pressure surges, such as those experienced during "kick-down" or "turbo lag" scenarios.

Important Notes:

  • Not suitable for: Diesel fuel and gasoline.
  • Pressure Handling: Can withstand varying pressures, making them ideal for use in systems where pressure surges may occur, such as in turbocharging or industrial machinery.

Stefsotra Silicone 135° Elbow Hose (L102*102mm) is the ideal choice for fluid transfer systems that require a 135° angle transition. Its high-quality silicone construction, reinforced with polyamide fabric, ensures exceptional flexibility, temperature resistance, and durability. Whether you're working with automotive cooling systems, HVAC systems, or industrial machinery, this silicone hose will provide reliable, long-lasting performance.
