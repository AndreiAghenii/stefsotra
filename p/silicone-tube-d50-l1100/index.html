Stefsotra Silicone Damper Hose L102 – Advanced Flexible Hose for Vibration Dampening and Fluid Transfer

Stefsotra silicone damper hoses (L102) are engineered using cutting-edge technology and made from high-stability silicone, designed to withstand repeated heating and cooling cycles without losing their flexibility. The interior of the hose is reinforced with high-strength polyamide fabric, providing added durability and resistance to wear and pressure. This combination of high-quality materials and advanced construction makes these hoses ideal for demanding applications in automotive, industrial, and heavy machinery systems.

Key Features:

  • High-Quality Silicone Construction: Manufactured from high-stability silicone, these hoses are designed for superior performance under repeated heating and cooling, making them ideal for applications that involve temperature fluctuations.
  • Polyamide Reinforcement: The interior of the hose features high-strength polyamide fabric, providing added durability and resistance to wear, pressure, and abrasion.
  • Temperature Resistance: Stefsotra silicone damper hoses maintain excellent performance in extreme temperature ranges from -60°C to +180°C, making them suitable for use in both cold and hot environments.
  • Vibration Dampening: These hoses are designed to absorb vibrations, making them ideal for use in applications that require movement or noise reduction, such as automotive and machinery systems.
  • Versatile Applications: Ideal for use in:
    • Cooling and Turbocharging Systems: Used in commercial vehicle cooling systems and turbocharging applications.
    • Additional Heating Systems: Suitable for mainline systems of additional heaters in vehicles and buses.
    • Hot and Cold Water Systems: For creating custom systems for heating and cooling in industrial, commercial, and residential applications.
    • Appliance Repair: Replacing outdated rubber hoses in household appliances with modern silicone alternatives.
    • Special Machinery Maintenance: Used in the repair of tracked and wheeled special equipment, including excavators and bulldozers.
    • Motorcycle and Buggy Construction: Perfect for motorcycle systems and custom buggy builds.
    • Vehicle Tuning: Ideal for automotive tuning, including sports and special-purpose vehicles.

Operating Conditions:

  • Extremely Durable: Stefsotra silicone damper hoses remain in excellent working condition in harsh conditions, from the extreme cold of northern regions to the intense heat of southern climates, such as the Kuban region.
  • Suitable for:
    • Weakly aggressive environments (coolant, antifreeze)
    • Cold and hot water systems

Installation: These hoses are easy to install and can be secured with standard hose clamps. They are ideal for use in applications where fluid flow control, vibration reduction, and temperature resistance are critical.

For reliable, durable, and flexible fluid transfer with added vibration dampening, Stefsotra silicone damper hoses (L102) are the perfect solution for a wide range of automotive, industrial, and heavy machinery applications.
