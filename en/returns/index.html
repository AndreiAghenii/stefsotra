Return policy

You may return a product within 30 days of delivery for a refund or an exchange.

At Stefsotra we aim to give our customers the best possible buying experience. If you are not fully satisfied with your purchase, you may return it within 30 days of the delivery date.

How a return works

  1. Write to us

    Contact customer service at stefsotra@mail.ru. Our team will guide you through the process and provide a return label where needed.

  2. Send the item back

    The item must be unused, in the same condition in which you received it, and in its original packaging.

  3. We inspect it

    Once we receive the returned item we inspect it to confirm that it meets the conditions of this policy.

  4. You are refunded

    If everything is in order we process the refund or exchange within 10 working days.

Details

Are shipping costs refunded?

No. If the item was shipped free of charge, the original shipping cost is deducted from the refund.

My item arrived damaged or defective.

Contact us immediately. We will resolve the problem with you and provide a replacement if required.

I bought from another retailer.

This policy applies only to products bought directly from Stefsotra. If you bought our product from a third-party retailer, please refer to their return policy.
