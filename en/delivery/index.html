Delivery

We deliver in Chișinău and across the regions of Moldova.

We cut hose to your required length at no extra charge — tell us the length you need when you send the request.

An order on this site is a request for a quotation, not a payment. We contact you to confirm availability, the final price and the delivery time before anything is dispatched.

Delivery options

Courier in Chișinău

200 lei, whatever the size of the order.

Post to the regions

For parcels under 5 kg. The cost depends on the delivery address.

Delivery to site

For large orders or delivery directly to a site, contact us and we will agree the terms.

Collection

You are welcome to collect. Call ahead so we can have the order ready.

Common questions

How long does delivery take?

We confirm the time when we answer your request. It depends on stock and on the delivery address.

Can I pay online?

No. This site does not take payment. An order is a request for a quotation and payment is arranged directly with your manager.

Do you deliver outside Moldova?

Contact us to discuss the terms.
