Stefsotra Rubber Cord NBR – Durable Sealing Solution for Industrial and Commercial Applications

Stefsotra Rubber Cord NBR is a high-performance sealing material made from Nitrile Butadiene Rubber (NBR), known for its outstanding resistance to oils, fuels, and petroleum-based substances. Available in diameters of 5 mm, 10 mm, and 15 mm, this round cross-section rubber cord is designed for sealing, vibration dampening, and protection against dust and dirt in various industrial environments. Ideal for use in stationary and disconnectable connections where sealing under pressure is required, it can withstand working pressures of up to 1.0 MPa.

Key Features:

  • Material: Made from Nitrile Butadiene Rubber (NBR), providing superior oil and fuel resistance for demanding industrial environments.
  • Diameter Options: Available in 5 mm, 10 mm, and 15 mm diameters, offering flexibility for various sealing and protection needs.
  • Pressure Resistance: Designed for sealing stationary connections under working pressures up to 1.0 MPa, ensuring a reliable and tight seal in systems exposed to pressure.
  • Dust and Dirt Protection: Effectively protects cavities and components from the ingress of dust, dirt, and other contaminants.
  • Temperature Resistance: Performs reliably within a temperature range of -40ºC to +100ºC, making it suitable for a wide range of applications in both hot and cold environments.
  • Abrasion and Ozone Resistance: Resists abrasion, cracking, and ozone degradation, ensuring long-lasting performance even under harsh conditions.
  • Versatile Applications: Ideal for seals, gaskets, dust protection, and vibration dampening in automotive, petrochemical, hydraulic systems, and machinery.
  • Flexible and Easy to Install: Its round cross-section and flexible nature allow for easy cutting and installation, providing a customizable fit for various sealing needs.

Stefsotra Rubber Cord NBR is perfect for use in stationary seals, disconnectable joints, engine gaskets, and protection against dust in industrial, automotive, and petrochemical applications. Its ability to withstand pressures of up to 1.0 MPa, along with its excellent oil and fuel resistance, makes it a reliable choice for sealing applications in systems with harsh working environments.
