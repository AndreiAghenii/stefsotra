Manufacturer description

The text below is the manufacturer’s and is not yet translated.

Stefsotra MK Coupling in brass – TW (Tankwagen) coupling to DIN 28450 for road-tanker and bulk liquid transfer. The MK part is the female coupling with a hose tail, clamped onto the hose and closing onto a VB or VK adapter of the same size.

Brass does not spark on impact, which is why it is specified for fuel, solvent and other flammable transfer where an aluminium or steel coupling is not permitted.
