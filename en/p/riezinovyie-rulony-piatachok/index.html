Rubber Roll Flooring "Pyatachok" – Durable and Reliable Anti-Slip Flooring

Rubber roll flooring "Pyatachok" is a high-quality and long-lasting solution designed to create safe, anti-slip surfaces in public spaces and vehicles. It is ideal for use in high-traffic areas such as entrances, stairways, lobbies, cafes, train stations, hotels, as well as in the salons of buses, trolleybuses, trams, and minibuses. This flooring is also widely used in industrial settings, garages, and livestock areas, providing safety and comfort in the workplace.

Features:

  • Material: 100% rubber.
  • Width: 120 mm.
  • Roll Length: 10 meters.
  • Thickness: 3 mm.
  • Price: Per meter.

Applications:

  • Public Spaces: Used in high-traffic areas, including entrances, lobbies, stairways, cafes, train stations, and hotels to prevent slipping.
  • Transportation: Perfect for use in public transport vehicles — buses, trolleybuses, trams, and minibuses.
  • Industry and Garages: Durable rubber flooring used in industrial settings and garages to provide safe, non-slip surfaces.
  • Livestock: Ideal for covering floors in animal housing areas, providing anti-slip protection and comfort for animals.

Advantages:

  • Durability and Longevity: Made from 100% rubber, ensuring a long service life.
  • Wear Resistance: High resistance to wear, making it perfect for high-traffic areas.
  • Anti-Slip Effect: The unique surface texture prevents slipping, ensuring safety in various conditions.
  • Versatility: Suitable for a wide range of applications, from public spaces to industrial and livestock areas.

Conclusion: Rubber flooring "Pyatachok" is a versatile and cost-effective choice for preventing slips in high-traffic areas. Combining durability, long-lasting performance, and excellent functionality, it ensures safety in a variety of environments.
