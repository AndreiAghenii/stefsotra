Stefsotra Porous Sponge Rubber EPDM – High-Performance Sealing, Insulating, and Vibration Dampening Solution

Stefsotra Porous Sponge Rubber EPDM is a versatile, high-quality material made from Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer (EPDM), a synthetic rubber known for its outstanding resistance to ozone, UV rays, weathering, and extreme temperatures. This porous rubber offers excellent sealing, thermal insulation, and vibration damping, making it an ideal solution for a wide range of industrial, automotive, and construction applications. It provides high soundproofing properties and is perfect for manufacturing soft sealing elements, shock absorption, and noise reduction in demanding environments.

What is EPDM?
EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer) is a type of porous rubber made from a combination of ethylene, propylene, and diene monomers. It is an ultra-lightweight rubber that offers great sound insulation, heat insulation, and vibration damping properties. With a temperature range of -30ºC to +95ºC, it is ideal for use in both low and high-temperature environments.

Key Features:

  • Excellent Durability: EPDM rubber resists weathering, ozone, UV rays, and extreme temperatures without losing flexibility, ensuring long-lasting performance even under harsh conditions.
  • Superior Sealing and Insulation: The porous structure provides effective sealing against air, water, and dust, while offering excellent thermal insulation to maintain temperature control.
  • Vibration and Noise Dampening: Its ability to absorb shocks and vibrations makes it ideal for automotive weatherstripping, industrial machinery, and other applications requiring noise reduction and impact protection.
  • Lightweight and Flexible: Despite its durable nature, EPDM is lightweight and flexible, allowing for easy installation in various shapes and sizes.
  • Wide Range of Applications: Commonly used for sealing, insulation, gaskets, vibration damping, automotive, construction, and electrical insulation.
  • Temperature Resistance: Performs reliably in temperatures from -30ºC to +95ºC, making it suitable for both extreme cold and hot environments.

Stefsotra Porous Sponge Rubber EPDM is ideal for creating soft sealing elements, vibration dampers, and thermal insulators in automotive, industrial, and construction applications, providing effective protection against noise, temperature fluctuations, and physical impacts.
