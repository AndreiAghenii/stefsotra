Stefsotra Paronite PMB – High-Performance Gasket Material for Sealing and Insulation in Harsh Environments

Stefsotra Paronite PMB is a high-quality asbestos-free gasket material, reinforced with rubber, designed to provide reliable sealing and insulation in industrial applications. Available in thicknesses of 0.5 mm, 0.8 mm, 1.0 mm, 1.5 mm, and 2.0 mm, Paronite PMB is widely used for sealing flange joints, pumps, engines, and other equipment in environments exposed to high temperatures, pressure, and aggressive chemicals.

This gasket material is perfect for applications requiring resistance to heat, pressure, and chemicals, making it ideal for industrial piping systems, hydraulic systems, and chemical processing environments. Stefsotra Paronite PMB ensures a tight seal even under extreme operating conditions, offering long-term performance and durability.

Key Features:

  • Thickness Options: Available in multiple thicknesses, including 0.5 mm, 0.8 mm, 1.0 mm, 1.5 mm, and 2.0 mm, for customizable sealing solutions.
  • Heat and Pressure Resistance: Designed to withstand high temperatures and pressure, making it ideal for use in engine gaskets, flange seals, and industrial systems.
  • Chemical Resistance: Highly resistant to a wide range of aggressive chemicals, including oils, solvents, and fuels, ensuring reliable performance in challenging environments.
  • Asbestos-Free: Manufactured without asbestos, providing an environmentally friendly and safer alternative for sealing applications.
  • Versatile Applications: Perfect for gaskets and seals in automotive engines, pumps, valves, pipes, and high-pressure systems in chemical, oil, gas, and hydraulic industries.
  • Long-Term Durability: Offers exceptional sealing performance and longevity, even in the most demanding conditions.
  • Customizable Fit: Easy to cut and shape for a precise fit in various sealing applications, ensuring high sealing efficiency.

Stefsotra Paronite PMB is ideal for use in flange joints and sealing connections where high temperature, pressure, and chemical resistance are critical. It is a preferred choice for applications in industries requiring robust, durable, and long-lasting sealing materials, such as petrochemical plants, power plants, and heavy machinery.
