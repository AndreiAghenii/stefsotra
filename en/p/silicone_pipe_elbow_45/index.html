Stefsotra Silicone 45° Elbow Hose – Durable and Flexible Fluid Transfer Solution

The Stefsotra Silicone 45° Elbow Hose is designed to provide reliable fluid transfer with a 45° angle transition. Made from high-quality, three-layer silicone with robust dacron reinforcement, this hose is a perfect budget-friendly solution for automotive cooling, turbocharging, and other fluid systems. The silicone construction offers flexibility and durability, ensuring it maintains performance even under high-temperature and high-pressure conditions. Its slight stretchability (up to 1-2mm) ensures a secure fit, and it’s compatible with hose clamps and connectors for easy installation.

Key Features:

  • Three-Layer Silicone Construction: The hose is made of high-quality silicone with a durable dacron reinforcement, providing enhanced strength, resistance to wear, and long-lasting performance in various automotive and industrial applications.
  • Flexible and Stretchable: The silicone hose can stretch slightly (1-2mm), allowing it to accommodate minor adjustments during installation. This flexibility makes it ideal for tight spaces and custom configurations.
  • Budget-Friendly Solution: Offering excellent performance at an affordable price, this silicone hose is an ideal choice for vehicle cooling systems, turbocharging, and other fluid transfer needs without compromising on quality.
  • Temperature and Pressure Resistance: Designed to operate in a wide range of temperatures and pressures, it’s suitable for both hot and cold fluid transfer systems.
  • Easy to Install: Recommended for use with power clamps of the correct diameter to ensure a secure, leak-free connection. Connectors and adapters are available for joining hoses together if necessary.

Applications:
Stefsotra Silicone 45° Elbow Hose is perfect for use in:

  • Automotive Cooling Systems: Ideal for use in engine cooling, radiator systems, and turbocharged vehicles.
  • Turbocharging Systems: Provides an efficient and flexible solution for fluid transfer in high-performance vehicles.
  • Custom Builds: Suitable for custom automotive builds and DIY projects, including off-road vehicles, racing cars, and modified engines.

Operating Conditions:

  • Temperature Range: Designed to withstand temperatures from -60°C to +180°C, making it suitable for both cold and hot water, coolant, and other fluids in various environments.
  • Pressure Resistance: Handles standard automotive pressures, ensuring reliable fluid flow in turbocharging systems and industrial applications.

Installation and Use:

  • Secure Fitting: The hose stretches slightly (1-2mm) during use, so it's important to use power clamps of the correct diameter for a secure, leak-free connection.
  • Connectors Available: If you need to connect multiple hoses, Stefsotra offers a variety of connectors and adapters to ensure seamless integration into your system.

Important Notes:

  • Stretchable Hose: The hose may stretch slightly (1-2mm) when in use, so proper fitting and hose clamps are essential for securing the connection.
  • Not Suitable for Diesel and Gasoline: The hose is not recommended for use with diesel fuel or gasoline.

Stefsotra Silicone 45° Elbow Hose is an excellent, cost-effective solution for automotive and industrial fluid systems, providing reliable performance and ease of installation. Its high-quality silicone and dacron reinforcement offer durability and flexibility for use in demanding environments.
