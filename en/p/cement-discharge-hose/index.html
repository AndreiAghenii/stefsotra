Stefsotra Polyurethane Aspiration Hose – Flexible, Durable Solution for Industrial Dust and Gas Extraction

Stefsotra Polyurethane Aspiration Hose is designed for high-performance air extraction and dust collection in various industrial applications. Manufactured from flexible and elastic polyurethane, this hose is reinforced with a steel spiral for added durability and strength. It is ideal for removing fibers, shavings, dust, powders, and gases, making it a reliable choice for demanding environments such as chemical processing, manufacturing, and central dust removal systems.

Key Features:

  • Flexible and Lightweight: The thin-walled construction provides a lightweight yet highly flexible hose that is easy to maneuver in industrial settings, reducing strain on equipment and operators.
  • Steel Spiral Reinforcement: The steel spiral reinforcement ensures exceptional strength and prevents crushing, even under high pressure or heavy-duty use.
  • Vibration Dampening: Excellent at absorbing vibrations, this aspiration hose is well-suited for use in mobile equipment, offering stability and preventing damage from mechanical shocks.
  • UV and Frost Resistance: Designed to withstand exposure to sunlight and cold temperatures, ensuring reliable outdoor performance in harsh conditions.
  • Gas and Chemical Vapor Resistance: Impervious to gases, it effectively removes chemical vapors, ensuring a safe and clean environment.
  • Static Discharge Capability: Capable of dissipating static electricity, reducing the risk of sparks in hazardous environments.

Applications:

  • Fiber and Shavings Extraction: Perfect for extracting wood chips, metal shavings, and other solid particulate matter in manufacturing and processing industries.
  • Dust and Powder Transport: Efficiently transports fine particles such as dust and powder in industries like pharmaceuticals, food processing, and chemical production.
  • Chemical Fume Removal: Used for venting gases and vapors in chemical plants, providing a safe extraction solution in environments dealing with volatile substances.
  • Centralized Dust and Fume Extraction: Ideal for industrial ventilation systems that require efficient dust and fume extraction, contributing to cleaner air and safer work conditions.

Benefits:

  • Durable and Long-Lasting: With its reinforced construction, the polyurethane aspiration hose offers extended service life, reducing the need for frequent replacements.
  • Flexible and Easy to Install: Its light weight and flexibility allow for easy installation and integration into existing systems, even in tight spaces.
  • Safe for Hazardous Environments: The hose’s resistance to gases, static discharge, UV rays, and cold temperatures makes it a versatile solution for a range of industrial applications.
  • Improved Operational Efficiency: The hose minimizes vibration, is resistant to crushing, and provides consistent, reliable performance under challenging conditions.
